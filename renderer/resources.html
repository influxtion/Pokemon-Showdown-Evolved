Everything you need to know to play

How Showdown works, what the format names mean, the rules each battle follows, and the chat commands worth knowing.

Getting started

  1. Pick a name. You can battle as a guest right away. Choose a name to keep it, and register it to protect it with a password and save your ladder rating.
  2. Start with Random Battle. Both players get six random Pokémon with ready-made sets, so you can play without building a team. It's the quickest way to learn matchups.
  3. Build a team. Most formats need your own team. Open the Teambuilder from the icon bar, choose a format, and add up to six Pokémon. Anything that breaks the format's rules is flagged before you battle.
  4. Battle and climb. Pick a format on Home and press Battle to find an opponent around your rating. Each format has its own ladder, rating and leaderboard.
  5. Practice without stakes. The Bots page lets you play against computer opponents. Those games never affect your rating.

Formats and tiers

Smogon sorts Pokémon into tiers by how often they're used. A Pokémon can be used in its own tier and any tier above it, so lower tiers ban everything stronger and stay balanced.

TierNameWhat it means
AGAnything GoesAlmost no rules. Every Pokémon, item and move is allowed.
UbersUbersThe legendaries and other Pokémon too strong for OU.
OUOverUsedThe standard competitive tier and the most played. The Pokémon used most in competitive battles.
UUUnderUsedPokémon not used enough to be OU. OU and Ubers Pokémon are banned.
RURarelyUsedThe next step down, banning everything in UU and above.
NUNeverUsedBelow RU, banning everything in RU and above.
PUPUBelow NU. The lowest tier Smogon ranks by usage.
ZUZeroUsedA community tier for the Pokémon left over below PU.
BLBorderlineToo strong for the tier below but not used enough for the tier above, such as UUBL. Banned from the lower tier.
LCLittle CupOnly first-stage Pokémon that can still evolve, at level 5.

Other popular formats

Random Battle
Six random Pokémon with sets chosen for you. No team needed.
Doubles OU
Two Pokémon on each side at once, with its own tier list.
VGC
The official Pokémon video game championship rules: doubles, bring six and pick four, level 50.
Battle Stadium Singles
The in-game ranked singles rules: bring six and pick three, level 50.
National Dex
OU-style battles that allow Pokémon and items not in the current games.
Monotype
Every Pokémon on your team shares one type.
1v1
Bring three Pokémon and use one. The battle ends when one side faints.

Clauses

Clauses are the extra rules a format adds on top of its ban list. The rules for each battle are listed at the top of the battle log when it starts.

Species Clause
A team can't have two of the same Pokémon.
Sleep Clause
You can only put one of your opponent's Pokémon to sleep at a time. Further sleep moves fail while it's asleep.
OHKO Clause
One-hit KO moves such as Fissure, Horn Drill, Guillotine and Sheer Cold are banned.
Evasion Clause
Moves that raise evasion, such as Double Team and Minimize, are banned. Many formats also ban evasion items and abilities.
Moody Clause
The ability Moody is banned.
Endless Battle Clause
Strategies that stop a battle from ever ending are banned, and every battle ends at turn 1000.
Team Preview
Both players see each other's six Pokémon before the battle and choose their lead.
Item Clause
Used in doubles formats such as VGC: no two Pokémon can hold the same item.

Chat commands

Type these in any chat, battle or private message. Words in capitals are what you fill in. In a battle you're playing, start a data command with ! instead of / to show the result to everyone.

People and messages

/msg USER, MESSAGESend a private message. /pm works too.
/r MESSAGEReply to the last person who messaged you.
/whois USERSee a user's rank and the rooms they're in.
/rank USERSee a user's ratings on every ladder they've played.
/ignore USERHide someone's messages. /unignore USER undoes it.
/away and /backMark yourself away or back.
/join ROOMJoin a chat room. /leave leaves the current one.

Pokémon data

/dt NAMEStats and details for a Pokémon, move, item, ability or nature.
/weakness POKÉMONWhat a Pokémon or type is weak and resistant to.
/effectiveness MOVE, POKÉMONHow effective a move or type is against a Pokémon.
/coverage MOVE, MOVE…The type coverage of a set of moves.
/learn POKÉMON, MOVEWhether a Pokémon can learn a move, and how.
/ds TERMSSearch Pokémon by type, tier, move, ability or stats, for example /ds fire, ou, fast.
/ms TERMSSearch moves the same way, for example /ms ground, physical, priority.
/randbats POKÉMONThe sets a Pokémon can get in Random Battle.
/smogdex POKÉMONA link to the Pokémon's Smogon analysis.
/statcalcWork out a stat from base stat, EVs, IVs and nature.

Battles

/challenge USER, FORMATChallenge someone to a battle in a format.
/timer onStart the battle timer so a player who stalls loses.
/forfeitGive up the battle.
/savereplayUpload the battle as a replay you can share.
/help COMMANDExplain any command. /help alone lists the common ones.

Ranks

The symbol before a name shows a user's rank, globally or in the current room. Staff are volunteers who keep chat friendly. /groups lists every rank.

+VoiceTrusted users who can broadcast commands to the room.
%DriverStaff who can warn and mute users.
@ModeratorStaff who can also ban users from a room.
#Room OwnerRuns a room and appoints its staff.
~AdministratorRuns the server.
*BotAn automated account that helps run a room.
☆PlayerOne of the two players in a battle room.
